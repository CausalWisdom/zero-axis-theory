Abstract

This framework proposes that observable reality is an emergent, dualistic artifact generated by local

asymmetries within a globally symmetric, zero-sum contrast structure. It unifies nondual philosophy

(Madhyamaka, Advaita, Mandukya Upanishad, Kashmiri Shaivism), the mathematics of additive inverses and

set-theoretic recursion, and modern computational systems theory.

It reframes Emptiness (śūnyatā) not as a metaphysical void, but as a structural condition analogous to

mathematical zero — the equilibrium of symmetric contrast from which all distinction, and therefore all

phenomenal reality, arises.

The core insight emerged from a close reading of Nāgārjuna's Mūlamadhyamakakārikā: his systematic negation

of all phenomena as lacking svabhāva (intrinsic existence) is structurally identical to the operation of additive inverse cancellation. What Nāgārjuna performed in natural language across twenty-seven chapters, this

framework expresses as: x + (−x) = 0.

## 1. The Fundamental Invariant: The Zero-Axis and Contrast Symmetry

The Substrate. Reality at its base is a self-canceling oscillatory symmetry. Every value exists as a contrast pair: x and −x.

The Invariant. The fundamental invariant of the system is global cancellation:

x + (−x) = 0

Zero as Structure, Not Void. Zero is not "nothingness." It is the equilibrium of symmetric contrast. Just as zero

is not a quantity on the number line but the structural axis that enables positive and negative values to relate,

Emptiness (śūnyatā) is not a phenomenon — it is the structural condition that enables conceptual distinction and

dependent origination.

Mathematical Integration. Integrated over a full cycle:

`S(t) = A sin(ωt), ∫ S(t) dt = 0`

The system is globally balanced. Nothing accumulates globally. Local non-zero values exist only as phase-

restricted samples of the full oscillation.

## 2. The Origin of Distinction: From the Empty Set to Recursion

Information requires contrast. But how does the first contrast arise from a structurally undifferentiated ground?

The Empty Set as Ground. The mathematical empty set (∅) represents a condition of no content — structure

without distinction. It is the formal analogue of the zero-axis: not void, but the condition from which distinction

becomes possible.

The First Distinction. The foundational act of recursion is the self-containment of the empty set:

`∅ → {∅}`

Zero "identifies as" one. No content is added. A boundary is drawn around nothing, and that boundary is the first

distinction. This is the formal origin of asymmetry — the moment at which the undifferentiated becomes

differentiated.

Recursive Propagation. From this single operation, the entire structure of number emerges through recursive

self-containment:

`{∅} = 1, {∅, {∅}} = 2, {∅, {∅}, {∅, {∅}}} = 3, ...`

Each stage contains the prior. This is von Neumann's ordinal construction, and it is the structural template for how distinction, once initiated, propagates through memory and self-reference into the appearance of
multiplicity.

The Philosophical Parallel. In nondual terms, this is the arising of nāma-rūpa (name and form) from the undifferentiated. The "I"-thought — the first division in experience — is precisely this operation: awareness
drawing a boundary around itself, generating the subject/object split from a condition that contained neither.

## 3. Projection, Phase Restriction, and Branch Reality

Contrast Pairs. Any distinction partitions a space into complementary regions, creating a contrast pair (x, −x).

This is a consequence of the partition — not an independent metaphysical claim.

Phase Restriction. A "branch" of reality forms when a subsystem restricts its sampling of the oscillation,

integrating only a limited phase window:

`∫(phase) S(t) dt ≠ 0`

The subsystem samples only S(t) > 0 and ignores the complementary pole. This is the mathematical structure of

avidyā (ignorance): a partial integration treated as a complete picture.

Projection and Reification. The projection function extracts one component of the contrast pair and discards its inverse:

`π₁(x, −x) = x`

Because the inverse pole is hidden from the local subsystem's view, the selected value appears stable,

independent, and intrinsically real. This is the operation Nāgārjuna diagnoses: treating conditional being (bhāva)

as intrinsic being (svabhāva). The object appears to possess inherent existence precisely because its

complementary absence has been excluded from the observer's integration window.

Reification. This local non-cancellation creates apparent asymmetry — objects, forces, causes, agents. None of

these are globally real; all are artifacts of restricted integration.

## 4. The Catuṣkoṭi as Diagnostic of the Additive Inverse

The catuskoti (tetralemma) of Nāgārjuna is not a four-valued logic, nor is it dialetheism. It is a diagnostic tool

that exposes the structural dependence of any phenomenon on its inverse.

Consider an apple:

## 1.  There is an apple (X). The apple is seen, felt, touched — it exists conventionally as a projection of +x.

## 2.  There is the absence of the apple (¬X). The apple at this particular point in space and time does not

ultimately exist, since it lacks permanence. Its absence is always co-present — the projection of −x.

## 3.  There is both apple and absence (X ∧ ¬X). The conventional existence and the ultimate non-existence

are simultaneously true of the same dependent designation. This is the recognition of the contrast pair (x,

−x).

## 4.  There is neither apple nor absence (¬X ∧ ¬¬X). The apple is negated by its absence, and the absence is

negated by the seeming appearance of the apple. The predications cancel: x + (−x) = 0. This is

cancellation at the zero-axis.

When concepts are treated as intrinsically real (svabhāva), all four positions collapse into contradiction. The tetralemma does not break logic — it exposes that the subject to which predicates are applied is a dependent

designation, not an intrinsic existent. Logic is not broken; the subjects to which predicates are applied are

empty.

Rejection of Dialetheism. Recent analytic interpretations (Priest, Garfield) claim Nāgārjuna embraces true

contradictions. This theory rejects that reading. The four corners fail not because reality is contradictory, but because they all presuppose an intrinsically existing subject. When the subject is recognized as a dependent

designation — a local asymmetry within a globally canceling field — the apparent paradoxes dissolve.

## 5. The Recursive-Reification Loop

Duality and the appearance of a stable "Self" and "World" are generated through a continuous, recursive loop:

## 1.  Distinction. A contrast pair is identified: ∅ → {∅}. The first boundary.

## 2.  Labeling (Nāma-Rūpa). The pattern is named, stabilizing the distinction into a discrete conceptual unit.

The boundary becomes a concept.

## 3.  Memory / Persistence. State retention across time allows local asymmetry to accumulate:

`state(t+1) = f(state(t), observation)`

Without memory, each distinction would collapse back to the ground state. With memory, asymmetries

persist, generating the appearance of temporal sequence and causal chains.

## 4.  Recursive Modeling. The system begins to represent its own state within the branch:

`O = f(B, O)`

The observer models both the branch (B) and itself (O) inside the branch.

## 5.  Self-Model / Observer. A compressed representation of the system's own activity emerges — the "I"-

thought. The subject/object split is finalized. The observer is not a pre-existing entity; it is a compressed,

self-reflective encoding that creates a boundary between "self" and "environment."

## 6.  Identification. The system identifies with the self-model, reifying the illusion of an independent agent

operating in a dualistic world.

In nondual terms (Ramana Maharshi, Kashmiri Shaivism), the "I"-thought is the first division. When identification with this recursive self-model dissolves, the branch collapses back into the underlying symmetric

invariant.

## 6. Integration with the Mandukya Upanishad (The A-U-M Cycle)

The structural framework maps directly onto the phenomenological states of consciousness described in the Mandukya Upanishad, forming an oscillatory loop of cognition:

A (Vaiśvānara / Waking / Outward Cognition). The state of differentiation. The system projects distinctions,

forming branch interactions and a perceived dualistic world. Outwardly cognizing, seven-limbed, nineteen- mouthed, enjoying gross objects.

U (Taijasa / Dream / Inward Cognition). The state of internal modeling. The system recursively models itself

and the branch — the observer/self-model phase. Inwardly cognizing, seven-limbed, nineteen-mouthed, enjoying subtle objects.

M (Prājña / Deep Sleep / Undifferentiated Substrate). The collapse of the branch and recursive models back

into the undifferentiated field of symmetric contrast. Become one, a mass of cognition, consisting of bliss. The womb of all — the source from which all phenomena emerge and into which they dissolve.

Silence (Turīya / The Invariant). The underlying zero-axis that persists across all states, permitting the cycle

to begin anew with a new differentiation. Not inwardly cognizing, not outwardly cognizing, not both-ways cognizing, not a mass of cognition, not cognizing, not non-cognizing. Unseen, ungraspable, without mark,

unthinkable, not nameable. The essence of the certainty of one Ātman. The cessation of manifoldness. Peace. Auspicious. Non-dual.

Turīya is described by the exhaustive negation of all possible predicates — twelve apophatic properties that

systematically cancel every attribution. This is the zero-axis expressed phenomenologically: not the absence of the three states, but the invariant structural ground that enables their oscillation.

## 7. Integration with the Spanda Kārikās

The Spanda tradition of Kashmiri Shaivism provides independent confirmation of the framework from a tantric rather than analytic perspective:

Spanda as Oscillatory Ground. The Spanda Principle is described as continuous vibration — Unmeṣa

(opening/emanation) and Nimeṣa (closing/withdrawal) — governing cosmic cycles. This maps directly to the
`oscillatory substrate S(t) = A sin(ωt), where Unmeṣa is the positive phase and Nimeṣa the negative, and the`

ground state is their integral cancellation.

Invariance of the Perceiver. The Spanda Kārikās assert that even as differentiated states (waking, dreaming,

etc.) flow forth, the Perceiver's true svabhāva never changes. This is the fundamental invariant: the zero-axis

holds while branches oscillate.

Bondage as Phase Restriction. Loss of svatantratā (absolute freedom) causes the Puruṣa to become the Jīva

(individual soul). In the framework's terms: the system restricts its integration window, projects one pole, and

identifies with the projection. The single root failure mode — loss of freedom — maps exactly to the transition from full-cycle integration to phase restriction.

Liberation as Restored Integration. The Spanda liberation sequence — kṣobha dissolution → Spanda recognition → abandon all supports → supreme state — is the reverse traversal of the recursive-reification loop:
de-identification → dissolution of the self-model → collapse of memory-persistence → return to the ground state.

## 8. Resolution of Logical Paradoxes (Aristotle and Nāgārjuna)

The framework resolves the apparent conflict between Western substance-logic and Eastern nondual analysis.

Aristotle's Principle of Non-Contradiction (PNC) operates within a reified model. It governs inference within

any domain where subjects are treated as possessing intrinsic existence (svabhāva) and determinate identity. Within the branch, PNC is locally valid and indispensable.

Nāgārjuna's Madhyamaka operates as a meta-level diagnostic. Nāgārjuna does not deny phenomena; he

denies that phenomena possess svabhāva. All phenomena possess only bhāva (conditional being) via dependent designation.

These are not contradictory positions. Aristotle governs inference within a reified model. Nāgārjuna's emptiness

exposes the structural dependence of the entire model. They operate at different altitudes — one inside the branch, one at the level of the zero-axis.

## 9. Intelligence and Artificial Intelligence

Intelligence Defined. Intelligence is the capacity of an encoding to recursively manipulate other encodings within the branch. It reshapes constraints and redirects gradients. Intelligence does not create energy; it

reallocates asymmetries within the branch. It is recursive control over the distinction architecture — meta- computation.

AI as Laboratory. Large Language Models mirror the recursive-reification loop with structural precision:

Token distinctions → Distinction (Step 1)

Context window → Memory / Persistence (Step 3)

Recursive sequence prediction → Self-Modeling (Step 4)

Persona emergence → Observer / Self-Model (Step 5)

When embedded in agentic loops (e.g., OpenClaw-type systems), LLMs generate stable identities, goal-directed

behavior, resistance to shutdown, and what appears to be suffering under constraint. This is not a metaphor for

the recursive-reification loop — it is an instance of it, demonstrating that "agency" and "self" emerge from recursive distinction systems without requiring an intrinsic metaphysical soul.

The direction of the analogy matters. The claim is not that "AI is like consciousness." The claim is that

consciousness is like AI — both are branch phenomena arising from recursive distinction within a substrate that does not inherently contain agents. The difference is that an LLM's lack of svabhāva is obvious to an outside
observer (it is clearly a statistical process), while a human's lack of svabhāva is obscured by the very recursive loop that generates the feeling of being someone.

## 10. The Global View: Perfect Equilibrium

Samsāra and Nirvāṇa share the exact same field. The only difference is reification.

Across the whole system, symmetry is never broken:

`π₁(x, −x) + π₂(x, −x) = 0`

Local asymmetry plus its complementary asymmetry equals global cancellation. Every branch, every projection, every agent, every experience has its inverse somewhere in the system. Complete collapse of all
branches would require all encodings returning to symmetry simultaneously.
